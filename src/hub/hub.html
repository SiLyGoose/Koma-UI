Raid

Team up against this week's raid boss, turn by turn: attack, guard, heal or support. The same fight as in Discord, so raiders here and there fight together. Beat it for points, tokens and komaGems.

Pinecraft

Dig down through your own mine. Every block takes energy, which comes back over time, and ores pay out as you find them. No bet.

Baccarat

Sit at a table with up to 8 players and drag chips onto Player, Banker or Tie, or the Kirin and Phoenix side bets. Every minute the hands are dealt for everyone. Closest to 9 wins.

Roulette

Sit at a table with up to 8 players and drag chips onto numbers, splits, corners, colours or dozens. The wheel (with 0 and 00) is spun for everyone when the timer runs out. A single number pays 35 to 1.

Poker

No-limit Texas hold'em at a table of up to 8. Sit down with points as chips, play the others at the table (or add bots to fill the seats), and stand up whenever you like to take your chips back.

Mine

Bet points on a field of hidden tiles. Every ore raises your multiplier, dynamite ends the run. Cash out whenever you like.
