Games

Weekly Boss

Team up against this week's boss, turn by turn: attack, guard, heal or support. The same fight as in Discord, so raiders here and there fight together. Your first win of the week pays five times over: zeiucoins, tokens, komaGems, hero essences and raid items.

TechnoMagic Raid

Galgoria, a three-headed chimera, and Siegfried, the corrupted King of Dawn, against your heroes, as in King's Raid: take one on alone, or get ready together in your server's room, each bringing heroes of your own, and fight it at once. Wins give TechnoMagic gear.

Dragon Raid

King's Raid's dragons (Temeraire, Scalaxia, Zyrrkinoth and Armarta) against your heroes, alone or together. Break their CC Gauge before their breath. Every win gives hero gear, zeiucoins and hero essences, with no daily limit.

Pinecraft

Dig down through your own mine. Every block takes energy, which comes back over time, and ores pay out as you find them. No bet.

Baccarat

Sit at a table with up to 8 players and drag chips onto Player, Banker or Tie, or the Kirin and Phoenix side bets. Every minute the hands are dealt for everyone. Closest to 9 wins.

Roulette

Sit at a table with up to 8 players and drag chips onto numbers, splits, corners, colours or dozens. The wheel (with 0 and 00) is spun for everyone when the timer runs out. A single number pays 35 to 1.

A♠K♥

Poker

No-limit Texas hold'em at a table of up to 8. Sit down with zeiucoins as chips, play the others at the table (or add bots to fill the seats), and stand up whenever you like to take your chips back.

Minesweeper

Clear one board together with everyone in the server, on a Beginner, Intermediate or Expert board. You can see each other's cursors as you go, and one mine ends it for all. No bet: just the clock.

Codedle

When a Codedle pops up in Discord, the vault is locked with a 5-digit code and everyone races to crack it, Wordle-style. Each guess costs a little and goes in the vault; the first to get it takes everything inside.

Mine

Bet zeiucoins on a field of hidden tiles. Every ore raises your multiplier, dynamite ends the run. Cash out whenever you like.
